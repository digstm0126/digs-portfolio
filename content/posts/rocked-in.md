**ProtoJam prototype · 1 week · Unity**

A 2D puzzle platformer where you don't control the jump, you control the ground. Use the mouse to build piles of rocks and help the character climb to the exit.

## What I learned

- Changing *what* the player controls can turn a familiar genre into a puzzle.
- A one-week jam forces you to find the fun fast and cut everything else.

**ProtoJam prototype · 1 week · Party game**

A Kahoot-style party game. Everyone in the room is given a fruit, and it's a race to see who can get theirs into the basket first.

## What I learned

- Joining on your own phone makes it easy for a whole room to play at once. This idea came back later in Patchwork Ōtautahi.
- In a party game, the fun comes as much from the people watching as from the players.
